Power cut checklist

Switch off appliances that could start by themselves when the power returns (cookers, heaters, irons).
Leave one light switched on so you know when the power is back.

Keep the fridge and freezer closed. A full freezer keeps food frozen for about 48 hours, a half-full one for about 24.

Never use a camping stove, barbecue or generator indoors: they give off carbon monoxide.

Check on neighbours who may need help, especially older people and anyone who depends on powered medical equipment.

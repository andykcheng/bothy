# Storing water

Keep at least **3 days** of drinking water for each person and pet: about 4 litres (1 US gallon) per
person per day, more in hot weather.

| Container | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sealed bottled water | Years | Keep it out of sunlight |
| Clean food-grade jerrycans | 6 months | Refill with fresh tap water twice a year |
| Bathtub filled before a storm | Washing, flushing | Not for drinking unless boiled |

If you are unsure whether stored water is safe, [boil it](boiling-water.md) first.
